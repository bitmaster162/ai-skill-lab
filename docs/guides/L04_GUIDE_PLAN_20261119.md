# L04 — AI Skill Lab, editorial guide №3: plan and evidence boundary

Status: DRAFT PR candidate; no published route, no merge, no deploy.

Primary language RU; EN translates the same facts and examples.

## Reader and thesis
Audience: small business owner, manager or team leader with no dedicated AI operations staff.
Question: How to adopt AI for one useful team task without treating a plausible draft as a verified business outcome?
Thesis: Start with one low-stakes document task, approved data, a named reviewer, repeatable checks and an explicit go/revise/stop decision.

## Outline
1. Pick one testable workflow and answer five questions about input, output, acceptance, owner and boundaries.
2. Training exercise table: meeting planning, process email, public-options comparison and draft internal FAQ; no real-client narrative.
3. Data use: categories, anonymisation limits, provider controls, secrets, permission to process third-party content.
4. Roles and review: prompter, verifier, owner of any outbound action, sources and correction.
5. Bounded pilot: record normal baseline, permitted data, time spent, quality and newly introduced errors.
6. Reusable prompt and teach-back exercise for colleagues.
7. Decision: expand, refine or stop, without promising ROI, jobs replaced or automation safety.
8. Source list and methodological limitations.
