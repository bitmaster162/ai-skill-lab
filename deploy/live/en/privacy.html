Privacy

Minimal data.
Clear contact path.

The current public launch operates in contact-only mode.

What the website collects directly

There is no first-party application form, account registration or checkout. The website does not ask for a child's name, phone, email or messenger details.

Telegram

Contact buttons open an external Telegram service. After leaving this website, data handling is governed by the external service and by the information the user chooses to send.

Technical data

The website is hosted on Vercel. The hosting provider may process standard technical connection data necessary to deliver and protect the website under its own documentation and privacy policy.

Minors

We do not ask children to submit contact details independently. Program coordination for a minor goes through a parent or another adult authorized to arrange the learning.

If a first-party form or online checkout is enabled later, this policy must be updated before activation.
