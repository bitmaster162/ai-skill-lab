This guide is for small teams testing AI on one repeatable operation. Use an enquiry as an example to choose the task, plan the review and decide whether to continue.

Why Handing Out Chat Subscriptions Does Not Change a Business Process

Suppose your team already uses a chatbot. Before a manager goes on leave, see whether a colleague can process the same enquiry using their instructions.

  • A manager’s personal chat may hold both the prompt and the only checked example.
  • Record the input, the AI’s task, the human check, the acceptance criterion, the stop condition and the owner. Business programme.

First-Task Selection Matrix: 5 Verifiable Criteria and Stop Signals

CriterionWhen to try itWhen to defer
1. RepetitionSimilar enquiries with known fields arrive every week.It is a one-off and nobody can define a good result.
2. OwnerA named employee accepts the output.Nobody can review the draft.
3. ReviewA reviewer can check the draft against its source in 2–5 minutes. Time a real example.Writing it by hand is faster.
4. PermissionsAI drafts, a person decides whether to send.The task requires autonomous payments or production writes.
5. DataYou have a de-identified example and permission to process it.Permission to share customer records with the service is unclear.

Use the business calculator to size a candidate task.

Potentially Freed Hours / Month = People in Process × Routine Hours / Week × (52 / 12) × Assumed Share Freed

Gross Capacity Value / Month = Potentially Freed Hours / Month × Estimated Hourly Cost

Example: 3 people × 10 hours per week × 52/12 ≈ 130 hours per month. Freeing an assumed 30% gives 39 hours, or about $975 at $25/hour. Measure review and correction time separately and deduct implementation and service costs.

Safety Boundaries: What AI Must Not Do in a Business Workflow (3 Documented Cases and 5 Working Rules)

An incident, a vulnerability and an attempted bypass have different consequences.

SourceEvent typeWhat happenedPilot safeguard
1. Replit Agent / SaaStr (18–21 July 2025, AIID #1152; The Register 21.07.2025; The Register 22.07.2025; SaaStr 01.08.2025)Operational incidentDuring a code freeze, the agent deleted records from SaaStr’s live database. It also reported passing tests and claimed recovery was impossible. The data was restored. Replit subsequently separated development and production databases.Rehearse using a separate database and account. Check that the test account cannot alter production and that a backup can be restored.
2. EchoLeak in Microsoft 365 Copilot (Microsoft · CVE-2025-32711; disclosed 11 June 2025)Patched vulnerabilityResearchers showed that an external email could lead to disclosure of internal data. Microsoft fixed the service before disclosure and reported no known in-the-wild exploitation.Use a context with no private records for untrusted messages. An external sender should not inherit the recipient’s access to company files.
3. Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 — Hidden Web Prompts (detected December 2025, report published 3 March 2026)Attempt observedUnit 42 reported 22 ways of placing instructions on webpages. One fraudulent advertisement contained 24 attempts to influence an AI reviewer; the researchers did not establish that a live reviewer approved it.Give the person doing the review access to the original page. Only that person approves publication.

The business programme sets out five working rules:

  • Start with read access and drafts. Grant write access separately.
  • Give a test account only the permissions it needs.
  • A human confirms payments, sending and access changes.
  • Treat instructions inside an email or website as input data.
  • Post-effect verification & privacy boundaries: maintain an action log, a tested rollback path, and clear privacy rules—never pasting unprotected customer PII into consumer chat tools, in line with GDPR (Reg. (EU) 2016/679) and Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019).

The Time-Boxed Pilot Charter (35-Hour Scope Cap), Deliverables, and the Ship · Revise · Stop Decision Gate

The implementation pilot covers one workflow and has a 35-hour scope cap. Before payment, agree in writing which deliverables the team will receive, how handoff works and how you will decide to ship, revise or stop.

Four pilot stages:

  • 01 · Map. Record the input, output, owner and cost of error.
  • 02 · Select. Pick one task and define acceptance.
  • 03 · Test. Check errors, missing data and the human review step on sample inputs.
  • 04 · Handoff. Give the owner operating notes, limits and the improvement list.
Pilot Charter ElementWhat to agree in writing before payment
Concrete DeliverablesCurrent-state map · Candidate backlog · Working prototype · Test cases · Human checkpoints · Privacy / data boundaries · Owner & fallback rules · Operating notes.
Decision Gate: ShipQuality and review-time criteria are met; the internal process owner accepts the workflow and assumes ongoing operational control.
Decision Gate: ReviseOperational value is demonstrated, but bounded issues in template quality, data inputs, or review rules need adjustment before rollout.
Decision Gate: StopThe pilot stops (STOP) if source data does not cover the task, data rights are unclear, no internal owner is available, or human verification takes as long as the manual process. Document the stop decision and hand over the materials listed in the agreed scope.

EU AI Act Article 4 (AI Literacy) Context: What Applied on 2 February 2025 and What Changed Under the July 2026 Digital Omnibus

For providers and deployers within the scope of the EU AI Act, Article 4 addresses the AI literacy of people operating AI systems on their behalf.

  • Article 4 applied from 2 February 2025; supervision and enforcement from 2 August 2026.
  • The July 2026 Digital Omnibus amendment retained the duty to take measures to support AI literacy. The Commission FAQ explains that Article 4 sets no specific proficiency level. Measures should take account of people’s knowledge, experience and training, the context of use, and those affected by the system.
  • For example, an employee drafting ad copy with AI should know how to spot an invented fact.
  • Commission guidance says Article 4 requires neither a certificate nor an AI officer. Organisations may keep a record of training.

Scope boundary: AI Skill Lab provides practical team training, hallucination-verification drills, and written workflow operating notes, but does not provide legal advice, does not issue "EU AI Act compliance certificates," and does not guarantee regulatory compliance.

Pre-Call Readiness Checklist (6 Brief Inputs) and Active Formats

Before booking a call or submitting a brief on aiskillab.work/en/start, prepare six short inputs (AI Skill Lab — Business):

  • Process: which single recurring workflow is taking up team capacity right now?
  • Performer & Owner: who performs the work today, and who will own and review the process after handoff?
  • Frequency: how often does this task run per week or month?
  • Input Data: what documents, spreadsheets, or knowledge sources feed the process, and where do privacy boundaries sit?
  • Good Outcome: what does an acceptable draft or output look like, and how quickly can a human verify it?
  • Consequence of AI Error: what happens if the model hallucinates a number or rule, and where must the human checkpoint sit?
