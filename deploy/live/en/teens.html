Curriculum

A 10-module map from literacy to portfolio.

Modules are a content map, not a one-module-per-session schedule. Explorer selects the essential blocks in 6 sessions; Portfolio covers the full core in 10; Builder adds 2 sessions for technical depth and project work.

01

AI literacy

Capabilities, limits and why outputs still need verification.

02

Research system

Sources, comparison and evidence-based conclusions.

03

Prompting as specification

Context, criteria, constraints and iteration.

04

Create & present

Writing, visuals and transparent authorship.

05

Code with AI

Read, modify and test simple code.

06

Automation

Turn repeatable steps into a workflow.

07

Product thinking

Problem, user, scenario and prototype.

08

Portfolio build

Build the project and document personal contribution.

09

Stress-test

Check edge cases, source quality and limitations.

10

Demo & next step

Present the work and map the next skill path.
