Adults · example 10-session core

From a task to a working AI process.

01

Diagnosis

Tasks, level, constraints and a useful success criterion.

02

Specification

Context, requirements, output format and quality criteria.

03

Research

Sources, fact-checking and uncertainty.

04

Reusable patterns

Templates and repeatable work scenarios.

Data & documents

Source material, context control and validation.

06

Automation

AI assistant

Youth · logic of a 10-session route

Understand → create → verify → explain.

1–2

Understand the tool

Capabilities, errors, context and why answers need checking.

3–4

Create

Ideas, writing, visuals or interaction while preserving original intent.

5–6

Research

Sources, facts, presentation structure and conclusions.

7–8

Build

Logic, a prototype, game or mini-product.

9–10

Defend the project

Final build, personal contribution and an independent presentation.

How progress is measured

Artifact + explanation.
Not prompt count.
