BEFORE PAYMENT

Commercial terms are confirmed in writing.

Website pricing is a reference for the stated package. The package states the number of sessions; the exact duration of each session is agreed and documented before payment together with the schedule. Before payment, the adult customer also receives confirmation of exact scope, currency and payment method, rescheduling, cancellation and refund terms. If agreed written individual terms differ from the website, the written terms take priority.

For business work, we first define scope from the six-field brief; commercial terms are confirmed in writing before payment.
