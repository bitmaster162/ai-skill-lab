Learn through the work.

Creator: 10 sessions → one finished project. Mini selects the essential introduction plus a small project; Studio adds 2 sessions for deeper project work. Themes adapt to the learner’s age and interests.

01

02

Better questions

Context, constraints and iterative improvement instead of one-shot prompts.

03

Visual worlds

04

Plot, characters, logic and editing while keeping authorship with the child.

05

06
